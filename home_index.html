Aapke sawaal, seedhe jawaab

Kya FinPick free hai?

Haan. Aapko koi advisory fee nahi deni. Premium wahi hai jo insurer charge karta hai. FinPick insurer se IRDAI-regulated commission leta hai — hum ye openly batate hain.

Mera data safe hai?

Aapki details sirf aapke quote ke liye use hoti hain. Hum data bechte nahi. Details sirf us insurer ke saath share hoti hain jo aap choose karte ho.

Claim kaise hogi?

Cashless: network hospital mein insurer/TPA desk pe pre-authorisation. Reimbursement: bills submit karke. Dono cases mein FinPick team documents aur follow-up mein free help karti hai.

Existing policy hai, kya karu?

Policy copy WhatsApp karo. Hum free review karke batayenge — gaps kya hain, top-up chahiye ya port karna better hai.

Online buy karu ya advisor se?

Dono options hain. Online compare karke khud buy karo, ya advisor se 15-minute call pe samjho. Koi push nahi.

Policy pasand nahi aayi toh?

Life aur health policies mein 30 din ka free-look period hota hai — terms pasand na aaye toh cancel kar sakte ho. Insurer proportionate risk premium, medical aur stamp duty charges kaat sakta hai.
